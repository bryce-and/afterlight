# U15 "The Ghost's Story" (v2.6): Gate B proposal (revision 2)

Status: **awaiting Bryce's approval.** Nothing is built. Chained release: U14 v2.5 (built, not yet pushed) and U15 v2.6 go out together.
Revision 2 replaces the first draft's story. Changes: the plot now follows real Destiny lore, the planets are reordered, and the Dreaming City is part of the campaign (U15b is folded in).

## 1. Objective

Turn the missions into one campaign with a Destiny-style story: a Ghost-narrated journey from the Cosmodrome to the Leviathan, with Vanguard and NPC comms, one destination open at a time, and real mission objectives. Add the Dreaming City as the campaign's seventh destination. Rebuild levels around the U13a systems. Reset everyone's progress at launch.

## 2. The story

### 2.1 Canon it stands on
- Emperor Calus once ruled the Cabal. Ghaul, Caiatl and others staged the Midnight Coup and exiled him and his followers aboard the Leviathan. In the Leviathan raid, Guardians accept his invitation and fight a robotic duplicate of him, not the man. ([Destinypedia: Calus](https://www.destinypedia.com/Special:MobileDiff/160326), [Destinypedia: Loyalists](https://www.destinypedia.com/Loyalists))
- Calus believes the Darkness chose him to herald its victory, and that this makes him a mentor figure to Guardians. ([Destiny Wiki: Calus](https://destiny.fandom.com/wiki/Calus))
- The Dreaming City is the Awoken Queen's hidden capital, infested by Oryx's Taken, who serve Riven, the Taken Ahamkara. Its areas include the Divalian Mists, Rheasilvia, the Strand, the Gardens of Esila and the Spine of Keres. ([Destiny Wiki: Dreaming City](https://destiny.fandom.com/wiki/Dreaming_City), [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/126308/tracing-the-stars-quest-destiny-2))
- Last Wish bosses Kalli and Shuro Chi are Taken Techeuns (Awoken). Our rule is that Awoken are never enemies, so they do not appear. Morgeth the Spirekeeper's species is not confirmed by my sources.

### 2.2 What is original (my inventions, all flagged)
- Ghaul falls on Mars, not in the Last City.
- Calus reaches out to the Guardian only after Ghaul dies.
- The Ghost's personal question: why did the Light choose this Guardian? Calus claims to know. The Ghost refuses his answer.
- Riven, heard only as a whisper (in the raid her siren speaks in familiar voices), tells the Guardian that Calus once made her a wish, and she gave him "a throne that cannot be defeated". This is our explanation of the robot duplicate. In canon the duplicate is just a duplicate.
- Riven herself is never fought here. She is saved for a future dungeon or raid. The dc3 boss is an original Ahamkara of her brood (see 3.4).
- Cabal gold coins stamped with the Emperor's seal turn up on early enemies. Pure foreshadowing.

### 2.3 The spine in four sentences
A Guardian wakes in the Cosmodrome and learns the system is waking up with them. They follow it from the Fallen to the Hive to the Cabal, and finish the Red Legion's war by killing Ghaul. Exiled Calus then sends an invitation and keeps sending gifts, while the Guardian deals with the rest of the Dark in Io, Titan, the Reef and the Dreaming City. When the Guardian finally boards the Leviathan, the Emperor they kill is an automaton, and the real one is somewhere deeper.

### 2.4 Voice and cast
The Guardian never speaks. The Ghost narrates in short text lines (dry, loyal, a bit scared). Other characters arrive as comms, because the Tower is U16: Zavala (steady, Titan's pride), Ikora (cryptic), Cayde-6 (jokes, alive throughout), Eris Morn (Moon, grief), Rasputin (Earth, Mars), Asher Mir (Io), Spider (Reef, schemer), Petra Venj (Reef, Dreaming City), Mara Sov (Dreaming City, speaks in riddles), Calus (via Psion messages, charming and cruel). Riven is heard only as a voice. Sample Ghost lines:
- "I dug you out of a pile of bones, you know. You're welcome."
- "That's a lot of Cabal gold for a Fallen with no ship."
- "He sent a gift. I don't like gifts."
- "I don't need him to tell me why I chose you. I remember."

One destination open at a time, as in Destiny 1. The last boss of a destination opens the next. Light values are retuned to run from 10 to 200 across the campaign (existing maps and bosses keep their names).

## 3. Game design

### 3.1 Objective system (new `js/objectives.js`)
Data-driven types plugged into `updateMission` (js/game.js:244): `reach`, `clear`, `boss`, `hold`, `activate` (hold F), `retrieve` (carry), `destroy`, `defend` (waves), `survive` (timed). Each sets `M.objective`, a HUD sub-line and a world marker. Co-op state goes in the snapshot (`ms`, js/coop.js:226). A zone without an objective stays `clear`.

### 3.2 Ghost dialogue (new `js/story.js`, plus a subtitle bar)
Lines keyed by trigger: mission start, zone enter, zone clear, objective step, boss phase, end. Text only, speaker label, a soft blip. Shown to everyone in co-op from the host's trigger (`{t:'say'}`). There is no dialogue system today.

### 3.3 Director
Story-gated: only the next destination is open; locked planets show a silhouette. Chapter title card and short Ghost recap on select. The linear `isUnlocked` (js/save.js:423) already gives the chain; reordering `LOCATIONS` in js/data.js and retuning Light does the rest.

### 3.4 Dreaming City (new destination, absorbs U15b)
New `LOCATIONS` entry, `js/level-dc.js` (three maps), themed props (Awoken ivory spires, crystal, mist, gardens), Taken-heavy zone plans via U14's `factions.js`. Zones are named after canon areas: Divalian Mists, Rheasilvia, the Strand, Gardens of Esila, Spine of Keres, the Wish chamber. Awoken appear only as NPC dialogue. Bosses use existing Taken models plus one new boss model: **Veshra, Riven's Brood**, an original young Ahamkara. Riven herself is reserved for a future dungeon or raid, and the Dreaming City is built so that fight can be added later. Use the `destinations` skill.

**Veshra's model (Destiny 2 Riven, scaled down).** From Destinypedia's description of Riven: a sleek quadruped with a long serpentine tail; a head covered by four bone plates that fold back to show fleshy undersides; a lower jaw split in two with rows of shark-like teeth; ten glowing eyes (Veshra gets six, so Riven stays the more terrible); three large spined tentacles at each side of the neck with smaller tentacles beneath; four-digit clawed feet; Taken mottling over the body. Sources do not say the form has wings, so Veshra has none. Her Blight heart is exposed in the fight, as Riven's is. Veshra is about a third of Riven's scale. Colors are not specified by my sources, so I will use dark mottled hide, a Taken orange glow, and the Dreaming City's gold, and compare against screenshots of the raid before locking it. Boss mechanics: plates fold open to expose the eyes and heart, tentacle slams, and Taken adds from the Blight.

### 3.5 Levels
Per R42/R43/R44: closed boss rooms (arena zones with `room`), interiors with real lighting, openable doors, stairs, platforms. **Traversal rule (all class jumps).** Every required route works with the plain ground jump, which every class has before its class jump. Measured from the code: gravity 24 and `jumpV` 8.2 (js/player.js:390, :1832) give a 1.4 m apex and a 0.68 s airtime, about 4.2 m of travel at walking speed. Required sections therefore use gaps of 3 m or less and rises of 1 m or less, with stairs or ramps for anything higher. Class jumps (Catapult Lift, Triple Jump, Burst Glide and their variants) can open optional shortcuts and a secret cache, never the only path. Blink users still use the ground path, so nothing is locked to them either. Tests: a reachability check that walks every required traversal against these limits, plus a play-check of each section with one class per jump type (lift, triple, glide).

New kit pieces: **door** (closed until triggered), **lighting beyond 6** (emissive glow plus a rotating pool of the nearest 6 real lights), and a collision pass (reproduce known AABB issues first).

### 3.6 Save
Save v9: `cleared` moves into each Guardian; campaign pointer and seen-dialogue flags per Guardian. **Full reset** at launch, old save backed up, settings kept. The scout found the in-game reset (`resetSave`, js/save.js:170) never writes a backup, so this update fixes that.

### 3.7 Cabal model pass (new, from Bryce)
**Problem (verified in code).** `cabal()` in js/enemies.js:258 builds the Legionary, Centurion and Colossus from one shared body: same legs, torso, helmet and arms, differing only in width (1.25x, 1.35x, 1.5x) plus small add-ons (a crest and cape on the Centurion, two ammo drums and a gatling on the Colossus). The Legionary and Centurion even share the same jetpack. At game distance they read as the same soldier at three sizes.

**Fix.** Each unit gets its own silhouette, helmet, armor kit and weapon, after its Destiny 1 version. Redesign from the silhouette outward, so each is readable at 40 m.

| Unit | Destiny 1 reference | New model |
|---|---|---|
| Legionary | Standard Red Legion soldier: stocky, bare armor, helmet with a single vertical visor, slug rifle | Smallest and plainest. Rounded helmet, light chest plate, no jetpack, slug rifle held two-handed, faction-colored shoulder stripe |
| Centurion | Officer with a plumed helm, heavy shoulder armor, and a back-mounted jetpack | Tall, with a crested helm, a cape, one massive pauldron, jetpack with visible thruster flame, a cannon in one hand |
| Colossus | Towering, wide, a huge head-less hulking silhouette, heavy gatling arm and a large armored back | Largest. Squat sunken helmet, a bulky chest and a huge armored gatling arm; the rear is a slab of plate and a gas tank; slower, wider stance |
| Phalanx (if present) | Heavy shield bearer | Clear tower shield and a lowered helm; confirm exists in the roster before building |
| War Beast | Four-legged hound | Keep, add a harness and spikes so it reads as Cabal |
| Psion | Small, hooded, floating | Keep the Psion Flayer boss; make the regular Psion a small hovering caster with a long robe |
| Incendior | Flamethrower unit | Add only if a unit slot exists; otherwise skip |

The bosses (Valus Ta'aurc, Bracus Zahn, Ghaul, Calus) already have their own models, so the Leviathan's Calus and his automaton reveal reuse that pass: an armored body plus a broken-open version. Cabal also gets a **distinct color accent per Red Legion**: the Red Legion keeps red, Calus's Loyalists get gold, so Mars and the Leviathan do not look alike.
**Cost.** Models are code-built boxes and cylinders (no assets), so each unit is roughly 60 lines. I will build them with the existing kit, test on the model viewer, and compare silhouettes side by side before and after. Haiku can draft the first pass of box dimensions; I review each before commit.

## 4. Scope and exclusions
In: Cabal model pass (section 3.7), objectives, Ghost lines, Director gating and reorder, Light retune, Dreaming City destination (3 maps, Riven boss model), Leviathan finale (1 map, Calus boss and reveal), level pass, save v9 and reset, Update Log, Roadmap, title card ("The Ghost's Story", "Version 2.6", new artwork). U15b is retired from the roadmap.
Out: voiced audio, the Tower (U16), patrol zones (U17), Legendary and Ultimate (U19), the real-Calus dungeon (U22), cutscene cameras beyond boss intros and the Calus reveal.

## 5. Risks
1. Size. Two new destinations (Dreaming City and Leviathan) on top of reworking 18 maps is the biggest update yet. Mitigation: build kit pieces first, prove them on Earth, then proceed in story order; the Dreaming City and the finale are built last.
2. Lore: I could not confirm Morgeth's species, and the Calus wish is invented.
3. Co-op sync of objectives and dialogue.
4. The light budget: `K.light` drops past 6 silently.
5. Reset is destructive; the old save must be backed up and tested.
6. Doors must block nav paths.

7. Cabal models: Destiny 1 references are from my memory, not checked against images; Bryce should confirm the silhouettes. Only the unit roster that exists in `enemies.js` (grunt, rusher, captain, heavy) gets rebuilt; extra units (Phalanx, Incendior, Psion) are a later addition unless Bryce asks.

## 8. Acceptance criteria
- A new save has only Earth open; each last-boss clear opens exactly the next destination; the Leviathan opens after the Dreaming City.
- Each mission has at least one non-clear objective; every boss fight is in a closed room.
- The Ghost speaks at the start, middle and end of every mission; the Guardian never speaks.
- The Leviathan ends on the automaton reveal and a campaign-clear screen.
- A v8 save is backed up (settings kept) and reset once; per-Guardian progress works.
- All tests pass, no console errors, Update Log shows v2.5 and v2.6, Roadmap is updated.
