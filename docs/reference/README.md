## Mars patrol zone (U17b, Bryce, 2026-10-24)

The images were sent in chat and are not saved in the repo (the attachments were not on disk to copy). What they show: Destiny 1 Buried City, tall rounded Golden Age towers with blue and rust panels, water tanks and red pipes, a curved railing and an OWR "M Line" transit station with wide steps; red dunes and cracked red rock under a hazy sky; a lone tower and a half-buried dome in the dunes; a Cabal base of huge ships on landing struts.

Brief: buried, condensed, tall human cities with some enterable interiors, a Halo Reach feel (fighting through a city); subway tunnels under the city; desert; a Cabal base or fortress outside the city; lots of verticality; intentional paths for the player on foot, and for larger distances by Sparrow.

## All patrol zones (Bryce, 2026-10-29)

Walkways, rails and platforms sit on what is under them: no floating rails, and a path over sloped ground follows the slope. Existing levels have this problem; a level QC pass is on the roadmap. New patrol zones must be built right from the start.

## Vex and Taken units (U17d, Bryce, 2026-11-02)

In `enemies/`, two images per unit (three for the Hydra), named `<faction>-<unit>-<n>`. Shape references: every unit uses its faction's one default look (Bryce, 2026-11-02; see `docs/U17d_PROPOSAL.md` Part 2), so the white, gold and blue variants (`vex-imp-swarm-2`, `vex-fanatic-1`, `vex-fanatic-2`) are built in the standard bronze.

- Vex: Harpy, Goblin, Minotaur, Cyclops, Hobgoblin (sniper), Imp Swarm, Fanatic (suicide), Wyvern, Hydra. The Supplicant is retired; the Fanatic replaces it.
- Taken: Thrall, Shadow Thrall, Acolyte, Vandal (sniper), Psion, Goblin, Wizard, Captain, Centurion, Phalanx, Knight, Ogre. The Taken Hobgoblin (sniper) has no reference: Vex Hobgoblin frame in the Taken style. The Taken Incendior is retired.

## Fallen units (U18, Bryce, 2026-11-05)

In `enemies/`, two images per unit, named `fallen-<unit>-<n>`: Dreg, Vandal, Captain, Brig, Shank, Explosive Shank, Servitor. Shape references. Colour (Bryce, 2026-11-05): every Fallen unit wears the red and bone of `fallen-vandal-*` and `fallen-captain-*` (bone plates, dark red undersuit, red cloth, blue eyes), whatever colour its reference shows; the Servitor keeps its own purple. Two more Captain images were sent in chat and not saved (the attachments were not on disk): a Captain holding a heavy scorch cannon with the upper pair of arms, spiked pauldrons, a fur collar under a scarf. The Captain carries that gun instead of the sword in `fallen-captain-1` and `-2`, because Captains shoot (Bryce, 2026-11-05). Line-up of the built models: `fallen-lineup-u18.png`.
