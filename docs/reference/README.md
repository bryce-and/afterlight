## Mars patrol zone (U17b, Bryce, 2026-10-24)

The images were sent in chat and are not saved in the repo (the attachments were not on disk to copy). What they show: Destiny 1 Buried City, tall rounded Golden Age towers with blue and rust panels, water tanks and red pipes, a curved railing and an OWR "M Line" transit station with wide steps; red dunes and cracked red rock under a hazy sky; a lone tower and a half-buried dome in the dunes; a Cabal base of huge ships on landing struts.

Brief: buried, condensed, tall human cities with some enterable interiors, a Halo Reach feel (fighting through a city); subway tunnels under the city; desert; a Cabal base or fortress outside the city; lots of verticality; intentional paths for the player on foot, and for larger distances by Sparrow.
