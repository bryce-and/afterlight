# Endgame activity rules

Bryce's ground rules for exotic missions, dungeons and raids (2026-11-12, set during U21b "Deep Whisper"). Every proposal and build for these activities follows them. Where a rule names only dungeons and raids, it does not apply to exotic missions.

## Traversal
- Traversal sections are long and need their mechanic (for example Attuned phase blocks). They are never a short hop.
- Gaps are measured against every standard class jump and glide (lifts, high, strafe and triple jumps, the three glides) at a typical Mobility of 50, a full legendary set. Exotics such as Stompees are not considered. Blink and Strand grapple are exempt: it is fine if they get around a section.
- Footholds do not have to be flat platforms: small wall ledges, floating rock, broken pillars, anything that fits the activity's theme.
- Enemies are placed throughout traversal sections, not only at the ends.
- Traversal can have moving geometry that knocks off players who do not watch their surroundings. Every mover has a readable telegraph and a safe timing window.
- Dungeon and raid traversal (and exotic missions where it fits) can hide a secret chest, sometimes found by completing a side mechanic.

## Enemies
- Enemies never walk, strafe or get pushed by each other off a ledge or platform.
- Players can still knock enemies off ledges with abilities and explosions.
- Enemies can leap between platforms along leap links the level declares.

## Puzzles and mechanics
- Every interaction with a puzzle or mechanic shows clearly whether it was right or wrong. The feedback does not have to be a light or a sound; a wrong answer can even be lethal (for example a wrong code kills the player). Lethal feedback is a design option, not a requirement.
- Symbols are large, and each one has a silhouette clearly different from the others at range. Draw them as shapes, never font characters.
- Always check that every symbol is readable from where players stand and is not blocked by or stuck in geometry.

## Bosses
- A boss with damage gates shows its health bar split into that many segments.
- Dungeon and raid bosses have no damage gates. They have DPS windows.
- Dungeon and raid final bosses always have an enrage, counted in DPS phases. Players get a warning first. Once the boss is enraged, it goes into final stand after the next DPS phase.
- Final stand (dungeons and raids): the last chunk of the boss's health. The boss starts a long wind-up with a dramatic animation that wipes the fireteam if it completes.
- Exotic missions do not need enrage or final stand.
